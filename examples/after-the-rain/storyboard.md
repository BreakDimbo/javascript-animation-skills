# 雨停之后 / After the Rain: storyboard

90 s, 3:4 portrait (1080×1440), 24 fps. 80 bpm in 4/4: a beat is 0.75 s, a bar is 3 s, and every cut falls on a beat.
The picture and the score both read the same `SHOTS`, `EVENTS`, `RAIN(t)` and `inside(t)` from the page.

Each shot is one continuous camera path over one painted plate, with the moving parts computed on top. There are no people: they are cropped out of the paintings. The only birds are the painted ones.

## Continuity

- **Weather:** `RAIN(t)` is 1 until 24 s and eases to 0 by 31.5 s. Rain, puddle rings, the wobble in the puddle and the rain in the mix all follow it. The sun breaks at 30 s, on the downbeat where the theme enters.
- **Light carries the cuts:** the window light becomes the light the bar opens out of; the roastery's window light blooms into the noon sun.
- **Direction carries the whips:** the promenade tilt keeps going down onto the table; the myna looks down and the camera follows it down to the neighbours' table.
- **Shape carries the match:** the latte from above and the latte from the side sit in the same place in frame.
