# 雨停之后 / After the Rain: storyboard

90 s, 16:9, 24 fps. 80 bpm in 4/4: one beat is 0.75 s, one bar is 3 s, and every cut falls on a beat.
The picture and the score both read the same `SHOTS`, `EVENTS`, `RAIN(t)` and `SUN(t)` from the page.

**Story in one line:** on a rainy morning by the Singapore River, a myna shelters under a café umbrella while a couple ducks in for coffee. The sun comes out, brunch arrives outside, and the myna steals a piece of the neighbours' muffin and flies off over the river.

## Continuity

- **Screen direction:** the myna faces left in the rain (toward the couple) and right in the sun (toward the tables). From the launch on, it moves left to right in every shot.
- **One perch, twice:** shots 3 and 13 are the same place, in rain and then in sun (damp wall vs. leaf shadows, wet feathers vs. sheen).
- **The weather is global:** `RAIN(t)` eases from 1 to about 0.1 between 21 and 27 s (the umbrella comes down), stays light through the interior, and ends at 45 s. `SUN(t)` rises from 45.4 to 48 s. Rain streaks, ripples, the rain bed in the mix and the glass rivulets all read these two curves.
- **The eyeline carries the cuts:** the myna looks down the path (end of 3), and the couple is there (4). It looks down at the table (end of 13), and we see what it sees (14).
