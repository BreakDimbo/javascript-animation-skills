# b8-swallow — 家燕 barn swallows + dream flight

## Exports (swallow.js) — stable
- `createSwallows(scene, planet={radius, camera}, opts={groups, alt})` → `{groups, lead, update}`
- `updateSwallows(dt)` — call every frame (also drives the dream flight when active)
- `playDreamFlight(fromDir, toDir, onDone, {camera, duration})` → `{cancel()}`; takes over the camera, paper-fade in/out; when it finishes the fade stays at 1, so the caller places the cat and then fades back with `setPaperFade(v)`
- `setDreamCamera(cam)`, `setPaperFade(v)`, `isDreaming()`

## What works
- Loose flocks circle the planet on great circles that slowly drift, 3–8 m up, with flap bursts and glides, banking, and radial-up orientation.
- The dream flight follows a great arc with an S-bend and climbs to 20–60 m. It uses a follow cam behind the lead bird and a paper-grain DOM fade (no glow).
- index.html: standalone demo with a painted equirect ground, clay trees and a "dream flight" button (`?dream` autostarts it).

## Not done / caveats
- The ground in index.html is demo-only (merge uses the real planet). The ground texture is still smoother than the designer's field painting.
- No collision with terrain; altitude is relative to R=600 (sphere), not to a heightfield.
- Port 8765 was serving /workspace/sheep/site during testing; I tested on `python3 -m http.server 8788` from /workspace/fold.
